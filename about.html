    About Us

    Origin

    This recipe didn't start as a business. At fifteen, it was learned from Vaidya ji in Bikaner — eight years of apprenticeship until the formulation became second nature. For decades after, it stayed a family ritual, made for the people around us, never sold. When the pandemic made everything uncertain, one thought stood out: this was something worth being proud to sell. Today it's made at our own facility in Sitapura, Jaipur — same recipe, just no longer only for us.
